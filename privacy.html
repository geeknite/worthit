Privacy Policy & Cookies

Last updated: September 2026

Data controller

This website (worthit.geeknite.com) is operated by geeknite.com. For any privacy-related enquiry, write to hello@geeknite.com.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no registration and no form that reaches us. The answers you give the tool are scored in your own browser and thrown away when you close the tab: the site writes nothing to local storage and keeps no record of the games you asked about.

Third-party services used on this site do collect information of their own:

  • Google AdSense: serves the ads on this site and may use cookies to tailor advertising to your interests.
  • Google Analytics 4: tells us which pages are read, from which country and on which kind of device, with IP anonymisation enabled. It does not tell us who you are. The same measurement id is used across the GeekNite sites, so a visit that continues on another one is a single visit rather than two strangers. Open any page with ?gn-analytics=off to switch it off.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the site and processes technical data (IP address, browser type) in its server logs.

This is the honest reading of the "no tracking" line on the home page: we do not track you, and the tool itself stores nothing at all -- but the ads that pay for the site are Google's, and the audience measurement is Google's too, and they set the cookies listed below. The measurement counts pages, countries and device types; it does not follow you anywhere, and ?gn-analytics=off turns it off for good.

Legal basis

Advertising and analytics cookies rely on your consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR). You may withdraw it at any time by reloading the page and rejecting cookies. For the analytics the withdrawal is ?gn-analytics=off on any page, which stops the measurement itself rather than only the cookie, on every GeekNite site at once; ?gn-analytics=on undoes it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing. Exercise them by emailing hello@geeknite.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Authority (AEPD).
